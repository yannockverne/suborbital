### Verse I

My Cutlass coughs before it flies,  
A duct tape wing and tired eyes.  
The HUD keeps screaming “system fail,”  
But I still jump, I never bail.

GrimHEX calls me every week,  
“You owe us parts, your ship’s still weak.”  
But I just smile, flip the switch,  
I’m broke but free — ain’t that rich?

### Chorus

My ship, my mess,  
A floating junkyard I confess.  
No shields, no plan, but I’m obsessed,  
She’s my love, my ship, my mess.

### Verse II

Got patched again in Lorville rain,  
She smells like fuel, she hums my name.  
They don’t let me near Orison’s line,  
Too much smoke for that skyline shine.

One day I’ll buy that 600i,  
With leather seats and screens that lie.  
But till that dream comes true I guess,  
It’s me and Drake — and this hot mess.

### Final Chorus

My ship, my mess,  
She groans but never loves me less.  
If space had rules, I’d still transgress —  
It’s my life, my ship, my mess.
