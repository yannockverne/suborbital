Writing and editorial

Narrative texts and editorial direction for SubOrbital Records were written and curated by Yannock Verne.

Tools and process

Some compositions were assisted by generative tools. Writing, arrangement choices, track ordering, mixing decisions, and narrative context were handled manually.

SubOrbital Records does not hide its tools. It documents its process.
