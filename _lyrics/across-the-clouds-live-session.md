### Verse I

Woke up on the clouds of Crusader sky,  
Selling dreams where the angels fly.  
Coffee’s cold, but the view’s just right,  
Got a ship full of joy, and a day to ignite!  
Cargo’s packed and the lights all green,  
Engines hum like a waking dream.  
Pull the throttle, feel it loud,  
We’re born to chase what moves the crowd!

### Chorus

We’re flyin’, yeah, across the clouds!

We build the ships, we ride the sound,  
Loreville shakes but we stand our ground!
