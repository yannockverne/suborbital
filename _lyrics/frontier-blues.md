### Verse I

Coffee cold, the dawn’s still red,  
Same sky, same debts, same thread.  
Took a haul from Shubin’s mine,  
Just me, the hum, and borrowed time.

They say the ‘Verse keeps turning fine,  
But out here, it don’t keep time.

### Chorus

I’ve got those frontier blues,  
Dust on my boots and nothing to lose.  
Stars don’t sing, they just refuse,  
To shine for me — frontier blues.

### Verse II

Heard a song on comms last night,  
Some drifter crying in the white.  
Maybe I’ll meet him down the line,  
Two ghosts that never intertwine.

They say the UEE’s got plans,  
But none were made for these hands.

### Chorus

I’ve got those frontier blues,  
Living on scraps, chasing truth.  
The sky don’t care, it just re-use,  
The dreams we burn — frontier blues.

### Spoken Bridge

“Somewhere out there, a prospector sleeps.  
Somewhere, a bounty dies.  
And me — I just keep flying  
until my ship forgets why.”

### Final Chorus

I’ve got those frontier blues,  
Stars above, the void below too.  
If freedom hurts, it’s nothing new —  
Just me and my tune, frontier blues.
