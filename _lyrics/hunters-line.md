### Verse I

Born under the smokestacks of Lorville,  
Breathing iron, learning stillness and steel.  
My ship was my ticket, my name was my will,  
No law could tell me what was real.  
Left the spires for the black between,  
Took my first mark out past Kareah’s gleam.  
GrimHEX lights and whispered deals,  
You learn fast — or you bleed for real.

### Pre-Chorus

Every job, another face erased,  
Every line, another scar misplaced.  
I swore I’d keep my soul in line,  
But the stars don’t care what’s yours or mine.

### Chorus

I walk the hunter’s line,  
Between the pay and the pain.  
Out in Pyro’s flame,  
No mercy, no name.  
From Ruin’s edge to the void divine,  
I walk the hunter’s line.

### Verse II

At Ruin Station, I found her eyes,  
A mark with fear that spoke of lies.  
She said, “We’re all prey in the same disguise.”  
And I froze — I heard my own reply.  
Left her drifting in the dark,  
Her voice still haunts my quantum sparks.  
Now every target looks the same,  
Another ghost that knows my name.

Until I cross the final line.

### Outro

Back to Lorville, same rust, same rain,  
But I don’t fit inside my name.  
The bounty’s paid, but I feel the cost,  
You can’t hunt ghosts and not get lost.

I walked the hunter’s line,  
Now I see through my own aim.  
No more marks, no more flame —  
Just a stranger wearing my name.  
From Ruin’s edge to the black behind,  
I lost myself on the hunter’s line.
