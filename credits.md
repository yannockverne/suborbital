    <div class="credits-prose"><p class="eyebrow">01 // YANNICK // THE PERSON</p><h2 id="yannick-title">The real person behind the signal</h2><p class="credits-opening">My name is <strong>Yannick</strong>.<br>Online, most people know me as <strong>Yannock</strong>.</p><p>Music has been part of my life for a long time, but never through a traditional path. I used to DJ, and my background came through electronic music, rap, sampling, rhythm and atmosphere rather than classical training.</p><p>I cannot read a score, and I have no formal background in music theory.</p><p>What I do have is years of listening, experimenting, starting again, and knowing when something finally sounds like what I had in mind.</p><p>AI-assisted and generative tools are part of the process behind SubOrbital Records, but they are not a replacement for creative direction.</p><div class="credits-versions"><p>Sometimes a track takes a few versions.</p><p>Sometimes eighteen.</p><p>Sometimes more.</p></div><p class="credits-statement">The tool can produce sound.<br>It cannot decide what SubOrbital Records is supposed to feel like.<br><strong>That part is mine.</strong></p></div>
  </div></section>

    <div class="credits-prose"><p class="credits-opening">For years, I have been documenting the <em>Star Citizen</em> universe through virtual photography.</p><p>Not simply as screenshots, but as photographs of places, ships, people and moments inside a world I have spent a great deal of time exploring.</p><p>That work naturally became part of my streams, community projects and my involvement with <strong>Imperial Geographic</strong>.</p><p>A lot of what appears in SubOrbital comes from that same world.</p><p>The photographs are real captures made inside <em>Star Citizen</em>. Many were made with the help of friends and members of the community.</p><p class="credits-statement">SubOrbital Records simply gave me another way to document the same universe.<br><strong>This time, through music.</strong></p>

  <section id="process" class="credits-section credits-process" aria-labelledby="process-title" data-rail-theme="light"><div class="shell credits-process-grid"><div><p class="eyebrow">METHOD // HUMAN DIRECTION</p><h2 id="process-title">Process</h2></div><div class="credits-prose"><p>SubOrbital Records combines human-led creative work with AI-assisted and generative tools.</p><p>The music is developed through generation, selection, rewriting, arrangement decisions, stem extraction, editing, mixing and mastering.</p><p>Narrative writing, lyrics, sequencing, artistic direction, visual identity and photography are human-led.</p><p>I do not present myself as a traditionally trained musician.</p><p class="credits-opening">I am someone with an idea, a set of tools, and an unreasonable willingness to make version nineteen when version eighteen still does not feel right.</p></div></div></section>
