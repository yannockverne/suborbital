<section class="card project-body">
<p>
  SubOrbital Records was born from a simple idea: capture music the same way we capture moments.
  Without strategy. Without expectation. Without polishing the edges that make them human.
</p>
<p>
  Recorded during a single week at The People’s Radio in Levski, SubOrbital is not a label in the
  traditional sense. It is a collection, a snapshot, a shared breath.
</p>

<h2>How it was made</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Recorded at The People’s Radio, Levski</li>
  <li>One week, October 13 to 19, 2955</li>
  <li>No contracts, no predefined roles</li>
  <li>Music shaped by encounters, not algorithms</li>
</ul>

<h2>Tools and process</h2>
<p>
  Some compositions were assisted by generative tools. Writing, structure, arrangement, mixing,
  and narrative context were handled manually. SubOrbital does not hide its tools. It documents its process.
</p>
</section>
