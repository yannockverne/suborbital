### Verse I

Fixed the leak with coffee grounds,  
Jumped to Pyro, lost my sound.  
HUD went dark but hey, she flies,  
Who needs screens when you’ve got the skies?  
Tried to land on Ruin’s pad,  
Half the ship said “nope, too bad.”  
The tower yelled, I hit reply,  
Then the radio fried — surprise!

### Chorus

She’s still my mess,  
But I swear she’s doing her best.  
One thruster left, one panel less,  
It’s love, it’s pain — it’s still my mess.

### Verse II

Ran from GrimHEX debt again,  
Said I’d pay, but when’s “when” again?  
Met a trader out at MIC L5,  
Sold him dreams — at least I tried.  
Now I’m grounded, broke, but proud,  
With a ship that hums way too loud.  
I toast to stars and curse the rest,  
Drake made a ship, I made a test.

### Chorus

She’s still my mess,  
But she never lets me rest.  
Each flight’s a gamble, each take-off’s stress,  
But hell, that’s love — and still my mess.

### Spoken Break

“Note to self:

### Final Chorus

She’s still my mess,  
Cracked hull and fearless finesse.  
From Hurston rain to Pyro’s stress,  
Till I crash, she’s still — my mess.
