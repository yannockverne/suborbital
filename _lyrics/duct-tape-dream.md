### Verse I

Woke up in orbit, coffee’s gone,  
Fuel gauge blinking, something’s wrong.  
The Corsair hums, or maybe groans,  
We patched her wings with hopes and bones.  
Who wired the nav to the coffee line?  
[female laughs in the background]

### Chorus

We’re flying on duct tape dreams,  
Laughing loud through the laser beams.  
If she breaks, we’ll make her sing,  
Drake built this mess — and gave it wings!

### Verse II

No shields, no plan, no clue at all,  
But we’ll land her soft — or not at all!  
Panels buzz, alarms in tune,  
Feels like payday, smells like doom.  
Nav’s offline, can’t find Pyro Five,  
Yet somehow we’re still alive.

### Chorus

We’re flying on duct tape dreams,  
Living loud in the in-betweens.  
If we crash, we’ll still be kings,  
Drake built this mess — and gave it wings!

### Bridge

A toast to bolts and broken glass,  
To every jump we didn’t pass!  
If the Verse don’t love us back,  
We’ll just repaint the hull in black!

### Final Chorus

We’re flying on duct tape dreams,  
Through fire, void, and static screams.  
If she burns, we’ll still be free —  
Drake built this mess — and gave it wings!

Drake made her hell, but home for me.  
Drake built this mess — and gave it wings!  
Drake made her hell, but home for me.
