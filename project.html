The Project

What is SubOrbital Records

SubOrbital Records was born from a simple idea: capture music the same way we capture moments. Without strategy. Without expectation. Without polishing the edges that make them human.

Recorded during a single week at The People’s Radio in Levski, SubOrbital is not a label in the traditional sense. It is a collection, a snapshot, a shared breath.

How it was made

  • Recorded at The People’s Radio, Levski
  • One week, October 13 to 19, 2955
  • No contracts, no predefined roles
  • Music shaped by encounters, not algorithms

Tools and process

Some compositions were assisted by generative tools. Writing, structure, arrangement, mixing, and narrative context were handled manually. SubOrbital does not hide its tools. It documents its process.
