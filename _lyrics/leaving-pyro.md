### Verse I

The firelight flickers through my windowpane,  
Another night, another name in vain.  
The city hums with broken dreams and scars,  
And every sunrise feels too far.  
They say in Pyro you learn to stand,  
But I’ve been learning how to land.  
I packed my bag, one breath, one spark,  
I’ll chase the dark beyond the dark.

### Pre-Chorus

And I hear the engines hum,  
In the void where hope comes undone.  
But there’s a lane beyond the war,  
Where the stars can mean so much more.

### Chorus

I’m leaving Pyro,  
Past the dust and the guns below.  
I’ve had enough of the crimson glow,  
I’m heading for Nyx where the stars still show.  
Leaving Pyro,  
Let the flames fade behind my soul.  
Maybe peace is just a place I’ll never know —  
But I’m leaving Pyro.

### Verse II

The gangs hold court where the shadows reign,  
They say we choose the chains we claim.  
I’ve seen the good hearts turn cold as steel,  
And I won’t pretend that this is real.  
The beacon’s weak, but I can see,  
A lonely lane of quantum dreams.  
If freedom costs my last regret,  
Then I’ll pay the fare and I won’t forget.

### Pre-Chorus II

And I feel the gravity pull,  
In a place where nothing’s real but the hull.  
Yet there’s a voice beyond the flame,  
Calling out my true name.

### Chorus

I’m leaving Pyro,  
Past the dust and the guns below.  
I’ve had enough of the crimson glow,  
I’m heading for Nyx where the stars still show.  
Leaving Pyro,  
Let the flames fade behind my soul.  
Maybe peace is just a dream I’ll carry on —  
But I’m leaving Pyro.

### Bridge

Maybe Nyx will never call my name,  
Maybe I’ll fade in stories of the same.  
But somewhere out there in the blue-void night,  
There’s a sky that still feels right.

### Final Chorus

I’m leaving Pyro,  
Past the dust and the endless glow.  
I’ll find my way through the undertow,  
To a sky I used to know.  
Leaving Pyro,  
I’ll keep my heart where the cool winds blow.  
Maybe peace is just a story we’ll tell tomorrow —  
But I’m leaving Pyro.
