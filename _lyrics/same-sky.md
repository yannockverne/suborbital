### Verse I

Coffee’s hot, engines hum in tune,  
Another day between the moons.  
Cargo stacked, the stars align,  
A little luck, we’ll make it fine.  
Check the charts, we’re good to fly,  
Another run beneath this sky.

### Chorus

We haul stars under the same sky,  
Different lives but the same goodbye.  
Every route, every name we try,  
Still the same sky.

### Verse II

Saw a ship held together by dreams,  
Still flying where the system screams.  
Crew of fools, but free and wild,  
They waved like kids, the stars all smiled.

She looked like silence shaped in chrome,  
Eyes like Pyro, but colder than home.  
One glance told a thousand tries,  
Somewhere behind her, something dies.

### Chorus

We haul stars under the same sky,  
Different hearts but the same why.  
Every drift, every lullaby,  
Still the same sky.

### Spoken Bridge

An old man in Orison said, “Keep your ship light.  
Heavy hearts don’t survive the night.”

### Final Chorus

We haul stars under the same sky,  
Every drift, every lullaby.  
Names may fade but we still try,  
Different ships, same sky.
