<section class="card project-body">
<p>
  It is not an album in the traditional sense.
  It is a gathering.
</p>

<p>
  During a journey across Stanton, Nok crossed paths with voices that rarely meet:
  pilots, drifters, mechanics, observers — artists without names, without plans,
  without any intention of being part of the same record.
</p>

<p>
  This compilation was born from that desire: to bring together distant horizons
  that were never meant to align, and to capture a shared moment rather than produce
  a polished release.
</p>

<p>
  Recorded over a single week at The People’s Radio in Levski, the project became
  something quieter and deeper than an album — a snapshot of encounters,
  a trace of a journey, a collective breath suspended in time.
</p>

<h2>What this project is</h2>
<ul>
  <li>A compilation of unknown voices encountered along the way</li>
  <li>A moment shaped by chance meetings, not coordination</li>
  <li>A shared experience rather than a finished product</li>
</ul>

<h2>Process and transparency</h2>
<p>
  Compositions were assisted by modern generative tools.
  Writing, structure, arrangement, mixing, and narrative context were handled manually.
  Nothing here is hidden.
</p>

<p>
  This project does not aim for legitimacy, performance, or perfection.
  It exists to document a moment — and let it exist as it is.
</p>
</section>
