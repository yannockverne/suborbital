PROJECT NOTE // TRANSPARENT RECORD

Behind the fiction

SubOrbital Records, its characters and its stories are fictional, inspired by the Star Citizen universe. The music was created with AI-assisted and generative music tools.

Writing, artistic direction, thematic choices, arrangement, editing, mixing and mastering are human-led. The photography and community participation are real.

This independent project is not affiliated with Cloud Imperium Games.
