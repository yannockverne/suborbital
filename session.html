The Session

Across the Clouds is not an album built to perform. It is a listening session. A collection of tracks born from shared time, shared space, and shared silence.

Recorded at The People’s Radio, Levski

Dates October 13 to 19, 2955

Format One week, no contracts, no predefined roles
