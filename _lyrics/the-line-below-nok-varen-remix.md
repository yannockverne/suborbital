### Verse I

Winds beneath my wings of steel,  
Ground below, stars to feel.  
Every turn a heartbeat line,  
Still alive, still aligned.

### Verse II

Down where silence meets the sand,  
Drift and trust what you can’t plan.  
Lightwave calling through the void,  
We don’t fall — we just enjoy.

### Outro

“Stay low. Stay free.”
