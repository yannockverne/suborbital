The Project

What is Across the Clouds

It is not an album in the traditional sense. It is a gathering.

During a journey across Stanton, Nok crossed paths with voices that rarely meet: pilots, drifters, mechanics, observers — artists without names, without plans, without any intention of being part of the same record.

This compilation was born from that desire: to bring together distant horizons that were never meant to align, and to capture a shared moment rather than produce a polished release.

Recorded over a single week at The People’s Radio in Levski, the project became something quieter and deeper than an album — a snapshot of encounters, a trace of a journey, a collective breath suspended in time.

What this project is

  • A compilation of unknown voices encountered along the way
  • A moment shaped by chance meetings, not coordination
  • A shared experience rather than a finished product

Process and transparency

Compositions were assisted by modern generative tools. Writing, structure, arrangement, mixing, and narrative context were handled manually. Nothing here is hidden.

This project does not aim for legitimacy, performance, or perfection. It exists to document a moment — and let it exist as it is.
